Origen e.V. Heidelberg - Hiking and Cycling Tours


ALL IN ONE: Combine Sport&Nature-Languages-Making Friends-New Cultures





Presentation:

Simple and Friendly

We hike and cycle on some sundays for a few hours in the Heidelberg region, eat and drink together, chat, and return home satisfied.

We are a non-profit group belonging to the association Origen e.V. Heidelberg.

You can participate one or two times to an event freely to test if it fits to you, but then you must become member of the association. The membership fee is only 15 EUR / year and can be resigned anytime per simple short letter (must be send before Sept 30th for the next year)

Important: As organizers, we take on a lot of work and responsibility with these events. As a thank you, please read our rules and confirm that you have read and agree to them when you register for the events. (See details in the 'Contact' section).


Concept

We organize short hiking and Cycling tours (2-3 hours) on some weekends. Registration is easy via a short email. See the exact procedure on the "Contact" section):

All explanations from the organizing team are in English,

but in small groups you can speak any language you like. Ideal for foreigners who want to integrate, learn German,

and make new friends. Also ideal for Germans who speak other languages ​​and want to experience new cultures. We have participants of all ages.

Our Rules:


1. The safety of every participant is our highest priority. Therefore, events may be canceled at short notice (1-2 days in advance) if the weather or other conditions are dangerous or unpleasant, or if there are insufficient participants. In this case, we will notify all registered participants by email and also announce it here on this website.

2. With registrating or participating, you agree to follow our rules described in the present section. Note that these rules are for you. They are important and are for your safety , for your health and for your well being. They make everybody in the group responsible. We reserve the right to exclude anyone who does not comply or who acts against the group's best interest.

3. For organizational and safety reasons, the number of participants is limited to 18 participants for hiking events and 12 participants for cycling events. The minimum age to participate is 18.

4. In case of an accident or serious injury, we call emergency services immediately. For all other situations, always carry the phone number of a trusted person who can help you. If you are able to get home on your own, please walk to the nearest bus or train station and use public transportation. Note that you do so at your own risk.

5. You travel to the starting point at your own risk. Also after the end point, it is also at your own risk. If you leave the group, you do so at your own risk, but you must inform the person in charge beforehand.

6. Do not participate if your physical condition or health does not permit it, or if you are ill or contagious.

7. Always act responsibly and do not endanger yourself or others.

8. Always wear appropriate clothing and footwear. Bring protective eyewear or other protective gear if necessary. Also, bring sufficient water and food for the event.

9. For cycling events: Cycling involves risks for you and others. You should have experience cycling on dry and wet roads. Wet roads are more dangerous because the risk of slipping is higher and braking effectiveness is reduced. Make sure you can adapt safely. Know and obey all German traffic regulations. Ensure your bicycle is in good working order: correct tire pressure, functioning brakes, and working lights. Wearing a helmet is strongly recommended for safety reasons. However, we do not actively check this, so each participant is responsible for their own helmet.)

10. Treat all participants with respect, regardless of origin, gender, nationality, sexual orientation, or other charact

11. Do not photograph other people without their consent. Do not publish photos from our events online or on social media. We will not publish them either.

12. Do not cancel your participation at short notice unless there is a genuine emergency or you are ill.

13. Insurance: Participation is at your own risk. Each participant is responsible for themselves and must have valid health and liability insurance in Germany. All applicable laws must be observed. applicable laws must be followed.
